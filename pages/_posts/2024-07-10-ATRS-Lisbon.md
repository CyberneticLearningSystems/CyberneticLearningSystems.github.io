Our group has had the privilege of presenting our paper "Causal Graph Neural Networks for Airline Profitability Prediction", with Julia representing us as speaker. Our approach represents a transition from standard black-box ML models to improved interpetability by modelling causal interrelationships rather than mere correlations. Through the quantification of the causal effects between the explanatory variables, the understanding of the mechanisms behind airline profit should be increased and a prediction of future profits based on current figures made possible.
